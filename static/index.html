Data and sources
  • Weather, forecast, rain probability: Open-Meteo model data, refreshed every 10 min.
  • Radar: RainViewer. Routing: OSRM (no live traffic). Map: © OpenStreetMap.
  • Official warnings: PAGASA's public page (Metro Manila) every 5 min, plus advisories entered by an administrator.
  • Reports and alerts: checked every minute while this page is open.
  • Flood-risk levels are estimates, not official warnings.
